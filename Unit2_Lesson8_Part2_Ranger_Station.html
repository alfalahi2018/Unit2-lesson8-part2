Lesson 8, Part 2 – Literacy: Reading

Grade 7 – Unit 2: Animals – Questions about an information text

Welcome to the Ranger Station, Junior Ranger! Check your field notes about the mata mata turtle and the giant prickly stick insect, then write your own report. Choose any channel to start – you can play the stages in any order!

👩‍🏫 Teacher's lesson plan (25 minutes)

Lesson aims: to read an information text and answer questions about it with full sentences.
Vocabulary: vertebrate, invertebrate, insect, reptile, strange.
Teacher's Book: Lesson 8, part 2, page 121 – Activity Book, Lesson 8 part 2 (exercises 1–4). Extra: photocopiable 20.
